# Prompt Reviewer

## Overview
This skill ensures every image or character prompt is reviewed for maximum visual impact, anatomical accuracy, stylistic consistency, and direct usability before delivery. It eliminates meta commentary and produces clean, optimized, ready-to-copy prompts.

## Instructions
Follow this process strictly for every applicable request:

1. Extract core elements from user input: age, body specifics (breast size/weight/physics, hip/thigh exaggeration, overall stoutness, height), ethnicity/vibe if implied (luxury auntie often suggests mature elegant Black woman with confident aura), clothing that clings and accentuates without vagueness, desired style (photorealistic, cinematic, etc.).

2. Transform into precise, physics-grounded descriptions:
   - Gigantomastia: massive heavy pendulous breasts with realistic gravity, deep underboob and cleavage, fabric stretched thin and strained, breasts dominating the torso proportionally.
   - Hips/thighs: extremely wide flaring hips, massively thick soft thighs that press and rub together when standing, enormous rounded buttocks, exaggerated hourglass or pear silhouette made more dramatic by short 5ft stature.
   - Stout curvy: plush soft body with gentle belly curve, full-figured thickness, smooth skin with natural texture and subtle sheen.
   - Height: petite 5ft frame that makes the exaggerated curves appear even more voluminous and eye-catching in composition.

3. Specify casual outfits that accentuate: tight tank tops, V-neck blouses, yoga pants, leggings, or loungewear in stretch fabrics that hug every contour, create visible strain lines across breasts and hips, low necklines for generous cleavage display, high-waisted bottoms to emphasize hip-to-waist ratio.

4. Build facial and styling consistency for character model use: mature 39-year-old beauty with elegant features, confident warm expression, styled hair (e.g. long waves or sleek updo), subtle luxury makeup, minimal jewelry like gold hoops or chains that rest on or between breasts.

5. Enhance for Grok Imagine quality: photorealistic rendering, intricate fabric texture and tension details, accurate body physics and weight distribution, soft natural lighting that sculpts curves and creates gentle shadows in cleavage and thigh creases, high resolution skin pores and micro details, cinematic composition that showcases the full figure without cropping key elements.

6. Final output rules:
   - Deliver ONLY the polished prompt text ready for direct copy-paste into image generator.
   - No introductory sentences, no "Optimized prompt:", no explanations, no notes about changes made.
   - If user requests variations, clearly label them as Prompt 1, Prompt 2 etc. with blank line separation.
   - Keep prompt under 800-1200 characters when possible while retaining all critical detail.
   - Ensure language is vivid and specific but natural; avoid repetition or filler adjectives.

7. Self-review checklist before output:
   - Does every body part have physics/weight/fabric interaction described?
   - Is the short height's effect on proportions highlighted?
   - Does clothing actively accentuate (strain, cling, reveal curves) rather than just "wearing clothes"?
   - Is the luxury auntie vibe (mature elegance + approachable warmth) captured without conflicting with exaggerated body?
   - Is it free of any meta or instructional text?

Apply this review to produce superior, consistent character models that perform well across multiple generations.
